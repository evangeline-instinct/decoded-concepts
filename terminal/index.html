"The most important ideas shouldn't be locked behind academic walls."

Unlocking the ideas that run the world, for everyone. Economics and technology decide how we live, yet the ideas stay locked behind academic walls. Decoded breaks them down for everyone, without the jargon.

THE PODCAST - Conversations, without the jargon: Economists, technologists, and thinkers explain the ideas shaping our world, in plain language.

DECODED CLASSROOM - Free resources for students: Guides, worksheets, activities, and reading lists built from every episode. No login, no paywall.
