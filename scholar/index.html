Preface

Unlocking the ideas that run the world, for everyone.

Economics and technology decide how we live, yet the ideas stay locked behind academic walls. Decoded breaks them down for everyone, without the jargon.

— The most important ideas shouldn't be locked behind academic walls.

THE PODCAST

Conversations, without the jargon

Economists, technologists, and thinkers explain the ideas shaping our world, in plain language.

DECODED CLASSROOM

Free resources for students

Guides, worksheets, activities, and reading lists built from every episode. No login, no paywall.

THE MOVEMENT

A youth-led effort to open access

Made almost entirely by young people, for people who were never handed the key. Bring it to your community.

Sundar Sarukkai and Akshat Bhaskar on Philosophy for Children, Technology, and What It Means to Be Human

Abstract. Philosopher and author Sundar Sarukkai on why his move from physics to philosophy was never a change of discipline, the barefoot philosophers project bringing philosophy to children in rural India, and why AI and personalized media wear down what Ambedkar called fraternity.

Conversation 2 · 29 Sept 2026 · 34 min

Cary Krosinsky on Whether the Financial System Can Fix Itself | Decoded x HERizon

Abstract. A conversation and live student Q&A with Professor Cary Krosinsky (Yale, Brown, NYU Stern; co-founder, Carbon Tracker Initiative) on the seven tribes of sustainable finance and whether the financial system can correct itself.

Conversation 3 · 29 Sept 2026 · 20 min

Decoded x HERizon: Sustainable Finance, ESG & Decarbonisation

Abstract. The associate team opens the Decoded x HERizon event: ESG as a risk lens, two trillion dollars of clean-energy finance, and decarbonisation in practice.

Conversation 4 · 11 Sept 2026 · 26 min

Prof. Sandeep Juneja: IIT, Stanford, COVID Modeling, and AI in Math

Abstract. Professor Sandeep Juneja (Ashoka University; formerly TIFR Mumbai) on modeling uncertainty across finance, epidemiology and climate, his COVID models for Mumbai, and what AI can and cannot yet do.

Conversation 5 · 01 Sept 2026 · 45 min

The Mathematics of Everything: M. Ram Murty on Numbers, Mind, and Meaning

Abstract. M. Ram Murty, Queen's Research Chair in Mathematics at Queen's University, on primes, the deep connection between mathematics and physical reality, and three hours a day set aside just to think.

Conversation 6 · 10 Jun 2025 · 50 min

From Wall Street to Startups: The Journey of Shiv Mohan Dutt

Abstract. Princeton Math '11 and Wall Street veteran Shiv Mohan Dutt on the trading floors of Goldman Sachs, burning out in finance, and building platforms like MeaVana and Yap Media.

Conversation 7 · 26 Feb 2025 · 25 min

Global Strategies & Financial Frontiers with Soumitra Mookherjee

Abstract. Soumitra Mookherjee (Cambridge, UCL, Professor at ISBF) on international marketing, financial planning, and global trade in an era of AI and blockchain.
